# Stash

Know where your stuff is.

Stash keeps track of what you own **and exactly where it is** — down to the
shelf wall, the compartment and the box inside it:

> **USB-C cable** — Left Kallax › 14th from the right, 2nd from the top ›
> Black box with lid · ×3
> — Right Kallax › 3rd from the left, 1st from the top · ×2

- One thing can live in **several places**, each with its own count and note.
- **Shelf walls** (a row of Kallax units, say) are a grid you tap on; you
  choose whether columns count from the left or the right.
- Rooms, areas ("behind the left Kallax") and boxes nest as deep as you like.
- Search forgives punctuation and case: `usbc`, `usb c` and `USB-C` all find
  the cable.
- **Import** a batch of things as JSON — a list made from a photo of a shelf,
  say — with a preview of what will be added before anything changes.
- A phone-friendly panel in the Home Assistant sidebar that follows your HA
  theme. Everything lives in one editable JSON file.

See [DOCS.md](./DOCS.md) for setup and the data format.
